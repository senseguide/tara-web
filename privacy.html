Šta prikupljamo

Gde se podaci čuvaju

Podaci o rođenju i partneru prvo se čuvaju lokalno na tvom uređaju. Ako se prijaviš, tvoj profil se sinhronizuje sa našom bazom (Supabase) da bi bio dostupan na više uređaja. Dnevnik i istorija izvlačenja čuvaju se samo kada si prijavljena.

Kome se prosleđuje (obrađivači)

  • DeepSeek: pisanje čitanja radi model ovog provajdera. Šaljemo mu tvoju natalnu kartu u tekstualnom obliku (položaji planeta, nakšatre, daše), izvedenu iz datuma, vremena i mesta rođenja. Ne šaljemo tvoje ime ni email. Tri izuzetka, jer bez njih te funkcije ne rade: kod čitanja za parove šalju se imena koja uneseš; kod mesečnog osvrta nekoliko odlomaka iz tvog dnevnika; a kada sama zatražiš osvrt na dnevnik, do 40 tvojih poslednjih zapisa, svaki skraćen na 300 znakova. Šta DeepSeek dalje radi sa tim podacima uređuje njihova politika privatnosti.
  • Supabase: baza i prijava (autentifikacija).
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): kad pretražuješ mesto rođenja, tekst koji kucaš šalje se radi pronalaska koordinata.
  • Expo / EAS: hosting i push-notifikacije.
  • Apple / Google: prijava i naplata pretplate preko prodavnica aplikacija.

Prenos van Evrope

Dva od gore navedenih obrađivača podatke obrađuju izvan Evropskog ekonomskog prostora. DeepSeek je kompanija sa sedištem u Kini i tamo obrađuje ono što mu pošaljemo. Mi sami poslujemo iz Srbije. Ni za jednu od te dve zemlje Evropska komisija nije donela odluku o adekvatnom nivou zaštite.

Podaci se ipak prenose zato što je bez tog prenosa nemoguće napisati čitanje koje si zatražila — to je jedina svrha, prenosi se samo ono što je gore navedeno, i to samo kada sama pokreneš čitanje. Ako želiš da tvoji podaci ne napuštaju Evropu, tu funkciju jednostavno nemoj koristiti; karta se i dalje računa na tvom uređaju.

Ako promenimo provajdera, izmenićemo ovaj odeljak i navesti novu zemlju.

Kada nekoga pozoveš

Kada pozoveš prijatelja, deli se samo link. Ti ne vidiš njegovu kartu, njegova čitanja ni njegov dnevnik, i on ne vidi tvoje. Nikada ne čuvamo podatke o rođenju osobe koja ih nije sama unela.

Pamtimo jedino da je nalog nastao preko tvog koda, da bismo znali koliko poziva je urodilo plodom. Iz toga ti se prikazuje samo broj.

Ime prijatelja stiže do tebe samo ako on to izričito dozvoli. Pitamo ga jednom, posle prijave, i ništa se ne šalje dok ne odgovori potvrdno. Tada se njegovo ime zabeleži u tom trenutku, pa kasnija izmena njegovog profila ne menja ono na šta je pristao. Ako odgovori odrično, ili ne odgovori, ostaje samo bezimeni broj. Ništa se ne prenosi u suprotnom smeru: prijatelj o tebi ne saznaje ništa.

Brisanjem naloga briše se i taj zapis.

Zašto (pravni osnov)

Podatke o rođenju i nalogu obrađujemo da bismo pružili uslugu koju si tražila (ugovor). Marketing (npr. novosti) šaljemo samo uz tvoju saglasnost, koju možeš povući u svakom trenutku.

Koliko dugo

IP adrese gostiju brišu se automatski posle sedam dana, bez obzira na sve ostalo.

Čuvamo tvoje podatke dok ne obrišeš nalog. Nalog možeš obrisati unutar aplikacije: Ti → Obriši nalog — time se trajno brišu tvoj profil, dnevnik i istorija. Lokalne podatke uklanjaš brisanjem aplikacije.

Tvoja prava

Imaš pravo da pristupiš svojim podacima, ispraviš ih, obrišeš, preuzmeš (prenosivost), povučeš saglasnost i uložiš pritužbu nadležnom organu za zaštitu podataka. Za bilo šta od ovoga piši nam na sroadstudio@gmail.com.

Deca

TĀRĀ nije namenjena osobama mlađim od 16 godina i svesno ne prikupljamo njihove podatke.

Bezbednost

Podaci se prenose šifrovano (HTTPS), a pristup bazi je ograničen pravilima na nivou reda (RLS) tako da vidiš samo svoje podatke.

Izmene

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

TĀRĀ writes you a daily reading from your real birth chart. To do that, we need your birth details. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and who it is shared with — in plain words.

Who we are

TĀRĀ is an astrology self-reflection app. The controller responsible for your data is:

S ROAD STUDIO
Popovica 225
21208 Sremska Kamenica, Serbia
sroadstudio@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Birth details: your name, date, time and place of birth (city, from which latitude/longitude and time zone are derived). Without these, the chart cannot be calculated.
  • Account: your email — only if you sign in (by email code, Apple or Google).
  • What you write: journal notes and any partner details you enter for compatibility.
  • Usage: which days you drew a card (for your streak), language choice, reminder setting and marketing consent.
  • Subscription: your TĀRĀ+ status. Card details are handled by Apple/Google and RevenueCat — we never see them.
  • IP address, guests only: if you ask for a reading without signing in, we record your IP address and how many readings it made that day. This is what stops a single visitor from consuming our AI without limit. It is tied to no account and deleted after seven days.

Where data is stored

Birth and partner details are first stored locally on your device. If you sign in, your profile syncs to our database (Supabase) so it is available across devices. Journal and draw history are stored only while you are signed in.

Who it is shared with (processors)

  • DeepSeek: the readings are written by this provider's model. We send it your birth chart in text form (planetary positions, nakshatras, dashas), derived from your date, time and place of birth. We do not send your name or email. Three exceptions, without which those features cannot work: couple readings include the first names you enter; the monthly look back includes a few excerpts from your journal; and when you ask for a reflection on your journal, up to 40 of your most recent notes, each trimmed to 300 characters. What DeepSeek then does with that data is governed by their privacy policy.
  • Supabase: database and authentication.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): when you search a birthplace, the text you type is sent to look up coordinates.
  • Expo / EAS: hosting and push notifications.
  • Apple / Google: sign-in and subscription billing via the app stores.

Transfers outside Europe

Two of the processors above handle data outside the European Economic Area. DeepSeek is based in China and processes there what we send it. We ourselves operate from Serbia. Neither country is covered by a European Commission adequacy decision.

The data is transferred nonetheless because without that transfer the reading you asked for cannot be written — that is its only purpose, only what is listed above is sent, and only when you start a reading yourself. If you would rather your data did not leave Europe, simply do not use that feature; your chart is still calculated on your device.

If we change provider, we will update this section and name the new country.

When you invite someone

When you invite a friend, only a link is shared. You do not see their chart, their readings or their journal, and they do not see yours. We never store birth data for a person who did not enter it themselves.

The only thing recorded is that an account arrived through your code, so that invites can be counted. All you are shown from it is a number.

A friend's name reaches you only if they explicitly allow it. We ask them once, after they sign up, and nothing is sent until they say yes. Their name is then recorded at that moment, so later edits to their profile do not change what they agreed to. If they decline, or never answer, only the nameless count remains. Nothing travels the other way: your friend learns nothing about you.

Deleting an account deletes that record too.

Why (legal basis)

We process your birth and account data to provide the service you asked for (contract). We send marketing (e.g. news) only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

How long

Guests’ IP addresses are deleted automatically after seven days, regardless of everything else.

We keep your data until you delete your account. You can delete your account inside the app: You → Delete account — this permanently removes your profile, journal and history. Local data is removed by deleting the app.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, export (portability) and withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. For any of these, email sroadstudio@gmail.com.

Children

TĀRĀ is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS), and database access is restricted by row-level security so you only see your own data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. We will announce significant changes in the app.
